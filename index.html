Prompt-Guided Biomedical Waste Classification using OpenCLIP + a Supervised Linear Probe

PUST · Independent Research · Jul 2026 — Present
Active research

Biomedical waste segregation is critical for infection control and sustainable healthcare waste management. This project frames the classification task as a low-compute transfer-learning problem: rather than fine-tuning a full vision-language foundation model, it keeps a pre-trained OpenCLIP encoder frozen and extracts semantic image embeddings guided by carefully engineered text prompts.

A lightweight supervised linear probe is then trained on those embeddings — cutting compute, training time, and GPU requirements while holding up well on small and medium-sized biomedical datasets.
